Our Story

We built VaultStorage because we believed storage should be simple, secure, and stress-free.

Our Beginnings

Built on a Simple Idea

VaultStorage began with a simple belief: storing your belongings should feel straightforward and secure. We set out to create a clean, well-maintained facility where people could find the space they need and feel confident leaving their belongings in our care.

They decided to build something different. VaultStorage opened with 120 units on a two-acre lot off Industrial Boulevard. The concept was straightforward: clean facilities, real security, and staff that actually answers the phone.

Fifteen years later, we've expanded to over 500 units across three buildings. The original principles haven't changed. We still sweep the hallways twice a week. We still answer every call. And we still believe that how we maintain the facility says everything about how we treat our renters.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Three principles that guide every decision we make, from building maintenance to customer service.

Security First

We continually improve our cameras, gates, and alarm systems. Your belongings are only as safe as the facility protecting them. We never cut corners on security.

Accessibility

Storage should work on your schedule, not ours. That's why we offer 24/7 gate access, drive-up units, wide hallways for dollies, and ground-floor options. Getting to your stuff should be easy.

Clear Communication

We explain our services, policies, and rental process clearly. Our team is here to answer questions and help you choose a unit that fits your needs.
