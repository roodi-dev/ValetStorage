Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from new and prospective renters.

Do I need to sign a long-term lease?

No. All our leases are month-to-month. You can cancel at any time with 10 days' notice.

What do I need to bring when I move in?

Just a valid government-issued ID. We'll handle the paperwork in about 15 minutes.

Is insurance required?

We strongly recommend it but don't require it. You can choose a tenant protection plan or provide proof of coverage from your homeowner's or renter's insurance policy.

Can I access my unit at night?

Yes. Gate access is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week using your personal access code. The entire facility is well-lit and monitored around the clock.

What can't I store in a unit?

No perishable food, hazardous materials, flammable liquids, live animals, or illegal items. We also don't allow anyone to live in a storage unit. A full list of prohibited items is included in the lease agreement.

Can I reserve a unit online?

Yes. Submit the reservation form and our team will confirm availability and help you with the next steps.

Prefer to Talk to Someone?

Give us a call during office hours. We're happy to answer questions, check availability, or walk you through the reservation process over the phone.
